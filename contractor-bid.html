Drawings & 3D model: angelfrn07.github.io/power-church-building

Project summary

Convert the four central auditoriums (houses 106, 107/IMAX-RPX area, 108, 109) into one combined worship sanctuary of ~12,100 sf seating ~1,000–1,500. The separation/screen walls between these houses are non-load-bearing and come out; all steel columns (17 within the sanctuary), exterior walls, and the roof remain. Work proceeds under the existing roof. This proposal covers Phase 1 only — the remaining ~50,000 sf (lobby/café, children's, classrooms, offices) is separate and may be phased.
