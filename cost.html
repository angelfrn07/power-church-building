Read it like this: building new is the lower, tighter number — but add land. Converting is a wider range because of the columns: keep the posts and it's cheaper; cut them all out for a fully open room and it climbs toward the top. For an honest apples-to-apples (a column-free worship room either way), it's roughly build ~$5–6M + land vs convert ~$6.5–8.5M, land included.

Neither is the automatic winner. Build new gives the cleanest open room for the money. Convert gives more than twice the building plus land, if the church wants the room to grow and can take on an older building.
